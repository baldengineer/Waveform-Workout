# Output A connection options

The v1 trainer plugs into a 1 MΩ scope input and has a button that users press while watching the waveform. For that use, try a locking BNC-to-terminal adapter in a printed cradle alongside the direct edge-mount plug. For a trainer that can rest on the bench, a captive coax pigtail is the simplest build.

| Connection | Parts and availability | Prototype check |
|---|---|---|
| Direct edge-mount male BNC (current J2) | HenryTech HL3-BNC-JFD-4-1, LCSC C54815072. Two available. [Drawing](https://datasheet.lcsc.com/datasheet/pdf/f3d1af0f565e3e068431c614c40f7cf6.pdf?productCode=C54815072) | Uses a 1.6 mm board edge. Check bayonet grip and adjacent-channel clearance before drawing the footprint. |
| BNC terminal adapter in a cradle | Adafruit 2888 was US$0.95, 39.4 mm long and 11 g. Delock 65323 is another option with 5 mm terminal pitch. LCSC stock unverified. [Adafruit 2888](https://www.adafruit.com/product/2888) · [Delock 65323](https://www.delock.com/produkt/65323/merkmale.html) | Capture the fixed adapter body, leave the bayonet collar free to rotate, and connect A and GND with short leads. Check grip, neighboring-channel clearance and the 2 µs pulse. Adafruit does not specify the impedance of this adapter. |
| Right-angle SMA jack and rigid adapter | LCSC C7498154 SMA jack (11,616 available) and C7420675 standard SMA-male to BNC-male adapter (5 available). [Jack](https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C7498154.html) · [Adapter drawing](https://datasheet.lcsc.com/datasheet/pdf/b3b71b0ade92d5a22c82ffb351313919.pdf?productCode=C7420675) | The 30.1 mm adapter increases the button's leverage on the scope jack. The same PCB connector also accepts an SMA cable. |
| Captive BNC pigtail | Cut one BAT WIRELESS 50 Ω RG-58 male-to-male lead into two short pigtails. LCSC C49563191 had 249 cables at about US$3.19 each, or US$1.60 per pigtail before labor. [Cable drawing](https://datasheet.lcsc.com/datasheet/pdf/2a6f0cf1dac547bd0af06c2556380d26.pdf?productCode=C49563191) | Solder center to output A after R5 and braid to GND. Strain-relieve the jacket and keep exposed conductors short. The board rests on the bench. |
| Right-angle female BNC and a bench cable | LCSC C20415789 jack (97 available) accepts a standard male-to-male BNC lead. A locking male-to-male barrel can also couple it directly to the scope. [Jack](https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C20415789.html) · [Amphenol 31-218-RFX barrel](https://www.digikey.com/en/products/detail/amphenol-rf/31-218-RFX/160252) | A bench cable keeps button force off the scope. A barrel adds length and a second bayonet joint. |

LCSC quantities and pricing were checked November 21, 2026. Use the scope's 1 MΩ input with each option. The 50 Ω rating describes the connectors and coax.
